XR Auto Service

The app

XR Auto Service shows a technician repair procedures on a Meta Quest 3, one step at a time. Each step has reference photos and safety warnings, and the technician takes a photo when it’s done. They can ask questions out loud without putting their tools down. The app works offline and syncs when the headset reconnects.

I built it end to end, from the headset app to the search behind the assistant.

Answering questions

Answers come from a knowledge graph I built from licensed service data. It links procedures, parts and specs. Every search is filtered to the year, model and engine of the car on the lift. Search combines keyword matching with a walk along the graph’s links.

Before building search, I wrote a toolkit that pulls only service data the company has the rights to use. It records the source and license of every document, and each answer cites its source.

Testing

Every change to search runs against questions written by repair experts, each paired with the procedure they expected. A model also grades the answers. When I checked the test itself, it flagged a broken ranker but gave several reasonable rankers about the same score.
