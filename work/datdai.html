DatDai · personal project · 2026

Question Answering over Vietnamese Land Law

An assistant that answers land-law questions in Vietnamese from the statute text and checks every article it cites against the passages it retrieved.

Role
Sole engineer, from parsing the statutes to deployment
Stack
Python, FastAPI, Gemini, Qdrant, Langfuse, Supabase Postgres, Google Cloud Run

The problem

Vietnam passed a new Land Law in 2024. Decrees issued in 2025 and 2026 have since amended the decrees that put it into practice, and one of those amending decrees has itself been amended. Someone asking about land prices or compensation needs the clause in force today, with an article number they can look up.

A plain retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) chatbot handles this badly. A retriever that matches on wording will quote a clause that a later decree replaced, and the answer reads just as confidently. The model can also cite an article it never saw.

What I built

DatDai answers from 11 documents, the 2024 Land Law and the ten decrees and resolutions that implement or amend it. I cut them into 4,366 chunks at article boundaries, breaking long articles at each clause. Each question then passes through five stages.

Retrieval runs dense and sparse search together in Qdrant and merges the two rankings with reciprocal rank fusion (RRF). A small Gemini model then scores each candidate passage between 0 and 1, and the top passages bring in the rest of their article so the generator sees whole provisions. The rewrite stage reads a short state kept for each conversation, so a follow-up such as “case 3” resolves to the third item of the previous answer.

An amendment graph records which decree amends or implements which. An index built by scanning the amending decrees links 24 amended articles to the chunks that rewrite them, and when a question asks how one decree changed another, the planner fetches those chunks alongside the search results. The citation verifier pulls every article reference out of the answer, with or without Vietnamese diacritics, and checks that each one appears in the retrieved passages. When one does not, the verifier logs it and appends a notice to the saved answer asking the reader to check the provisions it cites.

How I check it

Every answer built from retrieved passages goes through the citation check before it is saved. Each request is traced in Langfuse with one span per stage, and the trace carries scores that cost no model call, such as how many passages came back and the reranker’s top score. Readers can rate an answer with a thumbs up or down, and the rating lands on the same trace.

To judge answer quality, I wrote a script that pulls recent traces and has Gemini 2.5 Flash act as a judge at temperature 0. It scores each answer from 0 to 1 on whether it is grounded in the retrieved passages, and separately on relevance and completeness, then writes the scores back onto the trace. The judge works on questions people actually asked. It skips traces that already carry a score, so a rerun scores only new traffic.

Next
