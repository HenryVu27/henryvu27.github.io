GardenXR

What it does

GardenXR is a mixed reality plant-care app. You look at a plant through the headset’s passthrough view and capture it, and the app identifies the plant, checks its health and gives care tips. You can then ask follow-up questions by voice.

Getting camera access through review

Apps on Quest cannot see the camera feed by default. GardenXR depends on it, so before it could ship it had to pass Meta’s review for restricted camera access. It passed, and the app is live on the store.

An early version also ran an object detector on the headset itself. It spread inference across frames so it never stalled rendering, and projected the user’s pointing ray into the camera image to find the plant they meant. The released app identifies plants from a captured frame instead.
